# Product

## Register

product

## Users

The organizer runs a German tennis doubles tournament from a laptop or tablet during the SGA Sommerfest. They need one authoritative control surface for registration, matchmaking, round timing, results, and event history while people are waiting nearby.

Participants use a public read-only view on their phones through a QR code. They are usually standing around the club grounds, checking their current court, partner, opponents, and leaderboard without needing an account or app installation.

## Product Purpose

SGA Sommerfest coordinates a timed tennis doubles tournament for SG Arheilgen. It replaces manual pairing and announcements with a reliable organizer interface and a simple participant view.

Success means the organizer can run the event without technical help, players can quickly understand whether they are waiting or playing, and every round stays fair, visible, and easy to recover after refreshes or failed requests.

## Brand Personality

Sportlich, klar, vereinsnah.

The interface should feel practical and event-ready, with enough club identity to belong to SG Arheilgen Tennis without turning into a marketing site. It should carry a friendly local-sports atmosphere while staying readable under real tournament conditions.

## Anti-references

Avoid a corporate SaaS dashboard, dark gamer UI, or a childish cartoon-heavy Gartic clone. Avoid tennis-default green as a brand palette; SG Arheilgen Tennis should read as blue and white. The Gartic Phone lobby is a structural reference for the waiting-room topology, player slots, and host-led setup state, not a visual style to copy directly.

Avoid decorative card grids, overproduced landing-page sections, vague stock imagery, and playful illustration that makes the tournament controls feel less authoritative.

## Design Principles

Make the next tournament action obvious for the organizer.

Treat lobby and waiting states as first-class event moments, not empty placeholders.

Keep player identity quick to scan through names, emoji, rank, active state, and current court.

Use SG Arheilgen identity through the real club logo, blue-white club color, tennis context, and disciplined color rather than ornamental branding.

Design for outdoor, social, time-pressured use: large touch targets, explicit states, resilient German copy, and strong contrast.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast and keyboard/touch accessibility. Controls must have visible labels and not rely on color alone.

The public participant view must remain usable at 320 px width and above. Motion should respect reduced-motion preferences, and win indicators must retain accessible numeric labels.
